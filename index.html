    Research & Academic Interests

    My research interests sit at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and applied Machine Learning spanning biomedical AI reliability, renewable energy forecasting, and robustness under distribution shift.

    Artificial IntelligenceMachine LearningNatural Language Processing Retrieval-Augmented GenerationLLMsBiomedical AI Hallucination EvaluationRenewable Energy Forecasting Test-Time AdaptationDiffusion ModelsComputer VisionData Science
    Featured Publication Research Paper · Nutral Conference in Computer Science and Engineering (CompGineer)

    Biomedical Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Systematic Review of Hallucination Evaluation, Evidence Grounding, and Abstention

    A systematic review examining how Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems are evaluated for reliability in biomedical question answering surveying methods for measuring hallucination, grounding generated answers in retrieved evidence, and using selective abstention to avoid unsupported responses in high-stakes clinical contexts.

    Focus

    Hallucination evaluation methodology, evidence-grounding techniques, and abstention strategies across the biomedical RAG literature.

    Relevance

    Informs the design of more trustworthy AI systems for clinical and biomedical question answering, where incorrect or ungrounded answers carry real risk.
